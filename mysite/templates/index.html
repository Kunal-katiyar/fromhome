About

FromHome, at its core, is meant to give parents a greater connection to their kids when they're hours away. We know that parents are always busy, but the taste and feeling of home should never be too distant. FromHome allows parents to volunteer to send food to universities or send it without having to travel, making sure homemade food is available to all.

How to Use

If you're heading to the university in question, just select it from the dropdown and click "Add Trip." If you need to send something, simply tap on any available trip to register for it! You will recieve an email confirmation after submitting.
